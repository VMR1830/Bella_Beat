## **Objective**

The purpose of this study is to analyze non-Bellabeat smart devices' usage data to gain insight into how people are already using their devices. 
Using this information, we aim to produce high-level recommendations for how these trends can inform Bellabeat's marketing strategy. 
This will involve analyzing Fitbit smart device usage data to extract meaningful insights and applying these to Bellabeat's product offerings.

## **Business Questions**

1. What are some trends in smart device usage?
2. How could these trends apply to Bellabeat customers?
3. How could these trends help influence Bellabeat’s marketing strategy?

## **Data Source**

The data source used for this study is provided by MÖBIUS on Kaggle. 
This dataset was generated by respondents to a distributed survey via Amazon Mechanical Turk between 03.12.2016-05.12.2016. 
Thirty eligible Fitbit users consented to the submission of personal tracker data, including minute-level output for physical activity, heart rate, and sleep monitoring.

## **ROCCC Assessment**

- **Reliability**: The data is secondary and collected from only 30 participants with unclear demographics, so it may not be entirely reliable.
- **Originality**: The data is not original and was produced by Amazon Mechanical Turk.
- **Comprehensiveness**: The dataset includes 18 tables with minute-level details about participants' physical activity, heart rate, calories, and sleep, making it somewhat comprehensive.
- **Currentness**: The data is from 2016 and is 10 years old at the time of this analysis, thus not current.
- **Citation**: The data is cited with clear information about the author, title, and publication date.

## Conclusion

- Majority of users have a sedentary lifestyle, averaging below 5,000 steps per day, with most falling within the low to somewhat active range (5,000 to 9,999 steps per day).
- A small percentage of users are highly active, exceeding 12,500 steps per day.
- 17 users meet the WHO recommendation of 150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity activity per week.
-  However, none meet the minimum 150 minutes of moderate to vigorous activity when lightly active minutes are excluded.
- Higher activity intensity correlates with higher calorie expenditure, but most participants exhibit low to moderate activity levels,
-  indicating potential for improvement.
- 75% of participants record less than 10,198 steps per day, with half reporting more than 16 hours of sedentary activity per day.
- Peak activity levels occur between 17:00 to 19:00 and 12:00 to 14:00, while the least active periods are during late-night hours (01:00 to 04:00).
